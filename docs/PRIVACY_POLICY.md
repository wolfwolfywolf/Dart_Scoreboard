# Dart Scoreboard — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

This file mirrors the published policy at
https://wolfyspub.com/dart-scoreboard/privacy
(`site/dart-scoreboard/privacy/index.html`). Change both together.

Dart Scoreboard is a scorekeeping app for darts, published by WolfysPub Games.

## What the app collects

Nothing. Dart Scoreboard does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
information or usage data.

## What the app stores

The app keeps the following on your device only, so that games survive
closing the app:

- the names you type in for players,
- the scores and darts entered during games,
- a history of finished games.

This data never leaves your phone or tablet. It is not sent to us or to
anyone else. Clearing the app's history, uninstalling the app, or clearing
its storage in your device settings deletes it.

## Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone,
location, contacts, or network.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address
and the date above will be updated.
