## Copy blocks

**Meta description / one-liner**

> Wolfy's Derby is a horse racing dice game for 2–6 players. Draw a horse, ante up,
> and roll — first one home takes the pot.

**Short blurb (card, grid tile, ~40 words)**

> Six horses line up. You draw one at random, everyone antes a chip, and the dice do
> the rest. Every color that comes up moves that horse a space. First across the line
> takes the whole pot.

**Medium (product page intro, ~95 words)**

> Wolfy's Derby is a horse racing game you can teach in about thirty seconds. Each
> player draws a horse at random and antes a chip into the pot. On your turn you roll
> three dice — but these dice don't show pips, they show horses. Every color that
> comes up gallops one space closer to the finish, no matter who rolled. Because three
> dice land at once, two horses can cross together for a photo finish and split the
> pot. It plays just as well with a five-year-old as with a table of adults.

**Long (about page / full description)**

> Wolfy's Derby strips horse racing down to the part everyone actually likes: watching
> your horse run and having no control over it whatsoever.
>
> Six horses, one per lane, each a different color. You don't choose your horse — you
> roll for it, which means you're just as likely to end up on the one that comes last.
> Everyone antes a chip into the middle, and from there the dice run the race.
>
> The three dice are the trick. Instead of pips they carry the six horse colors, so a
> roll of blue-orange-orange moves the blue horse one space and the orange horse two. It
> doesn't matter who rolled — every horse named by a die moves. There are no decisions
> to make, which is exactly why a young child can play it on equal terms with an adult.
>
> With fewer than six players some horses go undrawn. They still run, and if one of them
> wins, nobody collects — the whole pot rolls over into the next race, which gets loud fast.

**Edition cards (one line each)**

> **Premium Edition — $56.99.** Custom dice, jockeys in racing silks, a fold-out rules folio
> and a retail box.
>
> **Standard Edition — $20.99, coming soon.** The same game with wooden color dice and classic

## How it plays (for a "how to play" section)

1. Stand each horse on its matching colored tile at the starting line.
2. Take turns rolling one die — the color you roll is your horse. If it's taken, roll
   again. Everyone antes one chip into the pot.
3. On your turn roll all three dice. Each die moves the horse of that color one space.
4. First horse across the FINISH line wins, and whoever drew it takes the pot.
5. If two horses cross on the same roll, they split it.

House rules worth mentioning: **Full Field** (2–3 players draw two horses each),
**High Stakes** (ante two chips), **Triple Crown** (best of three races).

## Brand

**Palette.** The lane colors are the backbone of the identity: board, dice and pawns. Use the
**Premium** set as the site's brand palette; use the Standard set only when showing the
Standard Edition.

| Lane | Premium | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#a8341f` red | `#262329` black |
| 2 | `#2f6ea5` blue | `#2a86cc` blue |
| 3 | `#c1902a` gold | `#f0bb14` yellow |
| 4 | `#c4621a` orange | `#ef7a14` orange |
| 5 | `#6d4a91` purple | `#8e54b8` purple |
| 6 | `#37773f` green | `#3f9e48` green |

Supporting colors: felt green `#4b6636`, cell green `#6d884b`, wood `#4a2a11` → `#a8723c`,
cream lettering `#f3e7c6`, gold rule `#e2c690`.

**Type.** A classic serif set in small caps with generous letter-spacing, cream on dark green,
with a soft dark emboss. Think old painted pub sign or vintage racetrack board, not modern
geometric sans.

**Visual direction.** Warm wood frame, mottled green felt, brass hardware, a brass horseshoe.
Drawn flat and bold rather than photographic.

**No checkered flags.** They're an auto-racing symbol, not a horse-racing one, and have been
removed from every board. The Premium box cover still has them; don't add the motif anywhere
new on the site.

**Accessibility.** Never rely on color alone. Premium horses also carry a number and a silks
pattern; Standard is color-only, so its six colors were chosen to be easy to tell apart across
a table. On the site, always name colors in text as well as showing them.

## Cautions

- **Ages.** The site uses **5+**, the owner's decision. The Game Crafter listing shows 14+
  because The Game Crafter only offers 14+ or 18+ (they don't publish games for children
  under 14). Don't "correct" the site to match the listing, and don't mention the listing's
  age setting.
- **Legal.** "Derby" on its own is a generic word and fine to use. Avoid anything implying a
  connection to the Kentucky Derby. Churchill Downs enforces that mark aggressively,
  including roses-and-juleps visual cues. The green and brass look is well clear of it; keep
  it that way.
