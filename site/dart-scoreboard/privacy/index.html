Dart Scoreboard Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Dart Scoreboard is a scorekeeping app for darts, published by WolfysPub Games.

What the app collects

Nothing. Dart Scoreboard does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

What the app stores

The app keeps the following on your device only, so that games survive closing the app:

  • the names you type in for players,
  • the scores and darts entered during games,
  • a history of finished games.

This data never leaves your phone or tablet. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's history, uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings deletes it.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or network. Android lists the standard internet permission that every app built with React Native carries; Dart Scoreboard never uses it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address and the date above will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email support@wolfyspub.com or see the support page.
