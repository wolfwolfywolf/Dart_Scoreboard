Common questions

Where are my games saved? On your device only. Finished games are under History & stats on the home screen. An unfinished game is kept and offered as Resume game.

How do I fix a wrong entry? Tap Undo on the keypad. It reverses the last dart or turn, even after a game has been won.

How do I edit or remove a player name? On the New game screen, long-press the name.

How does doubles work? Choose Doubles on the New game screen. Players are paired in the order added, share a score, and alternate throws.

What counts as a Shanghai? A single, a double and a triple of the round's number in one turn, in any order. On Bull it is two single bulls and one double bull.
